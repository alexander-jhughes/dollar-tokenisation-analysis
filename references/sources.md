# Further Reading

Sources relevant to extending this analysis, not cited in the current write-up.

## mBridge and CBDC settlement

- BIS Innovation Hub (2022, updated 2023) 'Project mBridge: Connecting Economies through CBDC'
- CBUAE CBDC Strategy report
- Primary source on the BIS's 2024 exit from mBridge and the launch of Project Agorá

## Petrodollar history, read critically

- Spiro, D.E. (1999) *The Hidden Hand of American Hegemony*, cited in this project's main analysis; the Saudi Treasury recycling chapter is the most directly relevant
- The 2024 "did the 50-year deal expire" reporting is worth weighing against the same critical lens as the 1974 exclusive-pricing story, most such reports overstate the existence of a formal treaty

## Stablecoins and dollar dominance, not yet engaged with

- IMF, BIS, and Federal Reserve working paper series on stablecoin macro-financial effects
- The GENIUS Act (signed July 2025) and its implications for dollar-linked stablecoin issuance
- Gulf regulatory frameworks (ADGM FSRA Stablecoins Rulebook, VARA, CBUAE payment-token rules) for a future comparison across GCC jurisdictions
