# Roadmap

## Completed

- USDC on-chain transaction cost and settlement speed analysis (Ethereum mainnet, 1,000 transactions)
- IMF COFER reserve composition econometrics: ADF stationarity, Granger causality, logit-transformed OLS substitution regression
- Delivery-versus-payment smart contract, tested and deployed to Sepolia

## Not pursued

- Deep learning comparison on macro-level series (COFER, stablecoin supply): sample sizes are too small (annual/quarterly, 40-80 points) for a neural network to offer any advantage over the classical baseline. Considered and deliberately excluded rather than attempted.
- Formal citation archival (CITATION.cff, Zenodo DOI): not proportionate for a portfolio project at this scope.
